A Candy Rain
Official Initiative

A Candy Rain: Simple Solar Technology

This initiative facilitates basic scientific understanding in underserved communities through the Seeds’ Town Panda Candy Jar Lamp—a tool for experiential science learning and safe lighting.

It addresses UN Sustainable Development Goals: Quality Education (SDG 4), Affordable and Clean Energy (SDG 7), Reduced Inequalities (SDG 10), and Climate Action (SDG 13).
